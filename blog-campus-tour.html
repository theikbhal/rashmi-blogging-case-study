Full Tour of DDU Gorakhpur University 2023-24

A campus walkthrough of Deen Dayal Upadhyay Gorakhpur University — the campus where Rashmi studies.

This guide covers a full tour of DDU Gorakhpur University for the 2023-24 session: the main gate, academic blocks, central library, hostels, sports, and everyday student life. It's the same campus where Rashmi Gupta studies B.A. Psychology between 4 PM and her evening shift running her blogging business.

What the Tour Covers

Main gate & location

  • The tour starts at the main gate of Gorakhpur University — an iconic landmark visible from the main road.
  • The campus sits ~2 km east of downtown, almost walking distance from the railway station.
  • Easy public transport makes daily commuting simple for students like Rashmi.

Academic blocks & departments

  • Faculties of Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Education, Engineering, Medicine and Agriculture.
  • 14 departments with modern classrooms and computer labs.
  • Department buildings like Pant Bhavan (Chemistry) and the Majithia Block (Physics) are highlights of any tour.

Central library

  • Over 100,000 books, journals, and e-resources.
  • Access to online journals via E-Pathshala and digital libraries.
  • For Rashmi, the library is a quiet place to study between classes — before she starts her 4 PM work shift.

Student Life & Facilities

Hostels & accommodation

  • Hostels for boys and girls with ~192 beds and attached washrooms.
  • Hostel fees ~₹49,000/year (single room): ₹30,000 hostel + ₹15,000 mess + ₹4,000 other.
  • Triple-sharing is cheaper (~₹39,000) — the kind of affordable living that keeps student costs low.
  • Rashmi's team actually started with roommates — hostel/flat life built her first writers.

Sports & wellness

  • Football, cricket, and basketball grounds plus a gymnasium.
  • On-campus medical center with emergency services.
  • A campus health center keeps student life healthy and productive.

Digital & innovation

  • Wi-Fi connectivity and computer centers across campus.
  • Research labs and startup incubation centers — relevant to student entrepreneurs.
  • 80+ MoUs with academic and business partners.

How This Connects to Rashmi's Story

Campus → schedule → business

  • Her college day runs ~6–7 hours; from 4 PM to 3–4 AM she runs her blogging business.
  • Hostel and roommates gave her the first team members — friends who saw her success and joined.
  • The library and campus facilities gave her quiet time to research topics and manage her sites.
  • Gorakhpur's affordable student life kept her costs low while profits grew.

The practical takeaway

  • You don't need a big-city campus to build a business — you need a schedule and a process.
  • A campus community is a built-in talent pool for your first hires.
  • Watching a campus tour helps you picture the environment where she balances study and work.
