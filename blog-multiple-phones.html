Blog Post · Topic Research · Google Discover

The Multiple Phones Trick

3–4 phones, one Discover feed each — the same topic on all of them is the signal to write.

Rashmi keeps 3–4 phones and checks Google Discover on all of them. When a topic like a new bike shows up again and again across devices, she writes about it immediately — and it trends. No expensive tools. Just a simple, repeatable way to find what's already getting traffic.

What She Actually Does

  • She keeps 3–4 phones with her.
  • On every phone, she opens Google Discover (the feed of trending articles).
  • She watches for a topic that appears repeatedly across all the phones.
  • Example: a new Yamaha bike keeps showing up in the Discover feed on all devices.
  • That repetition is the signal — she picks that topic, writes it, and it goes to Discover and trends.

Why This Works

Discover shows what's already trending

  • Google Discover surfaces articles that are already getting attention.
  • If the same topic shows up on multiple devices, it's not random — it's trending.
  • She's letting Google's own algorithm do the keyword research.

Repetition across devices removes doubt

  • One phone showing a topic could be an accident of personalization.
  • The same topic on 3–4 devices is a confirmed, repeatable trend.
  • Multiple phones = a free, visual signal-strength meter.

Speed is the whole game

  • Trending topics have a short window — write and publish fast.
  • Old articles sometimes resurface in Discover; spotting them early is the advantage.
  • This method feeds her 25–30 posts/day machine with topics already proven to work.

How to Copy This Trick

  • Use 2–3 devices (phone, tablet, spare phone) with Discover feeds open.
  • Check feeds at the same time daily — note any topic that repeats everywhere.
  • Pick the repeated topic, verify facts from official sources, and publish fast.
  • Log which picked topics trended, so you build your own signal history.
  • No budget? One phone plus checking Discover at different times works too.
