Start a News Blog in 4 Months

A new, step-by-step plan inspired by Rashmi Gupta's journey

Most people quit before Google notices them. This plan compresses Rashmi's 100-point roadmap into a realistic 4-month sprint — one idea, clear monthly targets, and the exact order of execution.

The Idea

Build a regional news blog for your district — in your local language.

Rashmi succeeded where others failed by serving a specific Hindi-speaking market (Gopalganj → Gorakhpur region). The same gap exists in thousands of Indian districts. Choose one district, cover its auto, education, and tech news daily, and become its trusted local source. It's specific enough to win early and big enough to grow into a news company.

Why 4 Months

  • Rashmi's Hindi site got its first real response after ~6 months; the English site failed in 4–5.
  • Google Discover and AdSense both reward months of consistency, not days.
  • 4 months is long enough to prove the idea and short enough to stay motivated.

The 4-Month Plan

Month 1 — Setup & Learn (Weeks 1–4)

  • Watch beginner tutorials; find a mentor or community (Google/YouTube).
  • Buy domain + hosting (WordPress) — pick a local language, not English.
  • Install RankMath, learn SEO basics and article structure.
  • Set up author profiles properly from day one (E-E-A-T ready).
  • Publish your first 10 articles; learn the publishing workflow.
  • Goal: a live site with 10+ articles and a repeatable process.

Month 2 — Consistency (Weeks 5–8)

  • Publish daily — 1–3 articles every day, no exceptions.
  • Ignore zero traffic; share every post in Facebook groups.
  • Build the habit before chasing results.
  • Study trending topics; keep a list of what your niche searches for.
  • Apply for AdSense once your site has solid content (expect rejections).
  • Goal: 30+ published articles and a daily publishing habit.

Month 3 — Content System (Weeks 9–12)

  • Watch Google Discover on 2–3 devices; spot repeated trending topics.
  • Research official websites for facts; draft in Google Docs.
  • Polish with ChatGPT, then verify facts before publishing.
  • Track what performs; remove weak topics; double down on winners.
  • Keep reapplying to AdSense after improving the site.
  • Goal: a repeatable research → draft → publish system producing 5+ articles/day.

Month 4 — Scale & First Results (Weeks 13–16)

  • Push toward 10–15 articles daily using your system.
  • If traffic grows, hire a friend or roommate part-time (pay per article).
  • Reinvest small profits into equipment or a better phone.
  • Expect Google Discover or AdSense to start responding — this is when most people quit, so keep going.
  • Goal: growing traffic, first monetization signals, and a trained writer (or two).

Rules That Keep This Working

  • Do not switch language or niche mid-plan — commit to one district.
  • Treat rejections (AdSense, zero traffic) as process feedback, not failure.
  • Verify every AI-generated fact against official sources.
  • One AdSense account per site; build real author profiles.
  • Track your numbers weekly — articles, topics, traffic, income.
