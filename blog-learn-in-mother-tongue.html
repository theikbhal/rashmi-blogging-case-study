Learn in Your Mother Tongue — Not English Alone

The first Hindi video is what actually got her started.

When Rashmi searched for blogging tutorials, the top video was in English — she couldn't follow it and moved on. The second video was in Hindi, and that's when everything clicked: "I can do this." Learning in your mother tongue isn't a shortcut — it's how real understanding and confidence start.

What She Actually Did

  • Searched Google and YouTube for how to start blogging.
  • The first search result was an English video — she skipped it because she didn't know English well.
  • The second video was in Hindi (Satish Kushwaha) — she watched it and finally understood setup, SEO, and RankMath.
  • From that point she believed, "Yes, I can do this."
  • She still reached out and DM'd her mentor when stuck — the language match made asking questions feel possible.

Why Language Builds Confidence

Understanding beats coverage

  • You can watch an English tutorial for hours and still not know why a setting matters.
  • In your mother tongue, the logic lands immediately — you absorb the why, not just the steps.
  • Confidence comes from understanding, not from finishing a video.

Who teaches matters

  • A teacher from the same region understands your context — costs, internet speeds, devices, and local opportunities.
  • You can ask questions, get replies, and stay in a community you understand.
  • Rashmi's mentor answered her DMs and guided her when she hit issues — a relationship language made possible.

Setup alone never made anyone money

  • Too many people finish the setup and stop — the real work is traffic.
  • Rashmi published daily, shared posts in Facebook groups, survived zero traffic, and waited for Google's response.
  • The setup is the start; consistent publishing and traffic are what matter. Read the full roadmap.

How to Apply This

  • Find teachers who speak your language — YouTube has deep content in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and more.
  • Watch in your mother tongue first; use English videos later to go deeper, not to start.
  • Join a community where you can ask questions without hesitation.
  • Remember: setup is 10% — traffic, consistency, and publishing are the other 90%.
