Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh

44.4 lakh people · Purvanchal's education hub · where Rashmi studies

Gorakhpur is one of the largest cities in eastern Uttar Pradesh and a major educational, transportation, healthcare, and commercial hub. For Rashmi Gupta, it is the city she moved to from Gopalganj, Bihar, to study B.A. Psychology while building her news blogging and content-writing business.

Why Gorakhpur Is Famous

Landmarks & heritage

  • Named after the saint Gorakhnath; the Gorakhnath Temple is its most recognized landmark.
  • Closely associated with writer Munshi Premchand, poet Firaq Gorakhpuri, and the mystic Kabir.
  • Headquarters of the North Eastern Railway and home to Geeta Press, the world's largest publisher of Hindu religious texts.

Education Hub

Why students come here

  • Gorakhpur attracts students from eastern Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and nearby Nepal.
  • Universities, degree colleges, medical and engineering colleges, coaching institutes, hostels and PG accommodation.
  • Per the district administration: 1 university, 1 technical university, 15 degree colleges, 135 higher secondary colleges.

Student life

  • Many students from nearby districts — including Gopalganj (Bihar) — move to Gorakhpur for higher education.
  • Better college options, affordable hostels, public transport, libraries, coaching centers, and part-time work opportunities.
  • Rashmi's case: she moved from Gopalganj, Bihar, to Gorakhpur for a BA in Psychology while simultaneously building her blogging and content-writing business.

Economy, Transport & Tourism

Economy

  • Major sectors: education, healthcare, retail, government services, railways, agriculture trade, and small industries.
  • Recent investment initiatives have expanded industrial activity in the region.

Transportation

  • Connected by national highways, the North Eastern Railway headquarters, a commercial airport, and state/interstate bus services.
  • Direct connectivity to Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Lucknow, and Varanasi.

Tourist attractions

  • Gorakhnath Temple, Geeta Press, Ramgarh Tal Lake, Railway Museum, Kushmi Forest, Veer Bahadur Singh Planetarium, Arogya Mandir, and Imambara.
  • Nearby places: Kushinagar, Maharajganj, Deoria, Sant Kabir Nagar, Basti, Siddharthnagar, and the Nepal border.

Why Students Choose Gorakhpur

  • More degree options.
  • Better educational infrastructure.
  • Affordable living compared with larger metropolitan cities.
  • Easier access to coaching and competitive exam preparation.
  • Opportunities to combine studies with freelancing or online work.

Rashmi Gupta Connection
