How She Writes Bike & Car Articles

The auto content formula behind trending articles like "Tata Harrier 2025."

Bikes and cars are a core category on her sites — auto topics repeatedly surface on Google Discover, so she writes them again and again. The formula: spot the new model on multiple phones, pull specs from the official website, write a click-worthy title with ChatGPT, verify every fact, and publish fast. Here's exactly how an auto article is made.

How an Auto Article Gets Made

  • A new bike or car (e.g., a Yamaha bike or Tata Harrier 2025) keeps appearing in her Discover feeds across 3–4 phones.
  • That repetition signals a trending auto topic — she picks it up.
  • She collects the details from the official website (the bike/car maker's site) — specs, price, features.
  • She drafts the article and uses ChatGPT to polish it and write the title.
  • She verifies the AI output against the official source — price, features, and details must match.
  • She publishes quickly while the topic is still trending.

The Title Formula

Click-worthy but factual

  • Example from her site: "Tata Harrier 2025: Style, Safety and Performance ka Damdaar Mel" (a strong blend of style, safety, and performance).
  • She gives ChatGPT the bike/car name and asks for attractive titles the audience will click.
  • She can tell ChatGPT to add price and features to the title — it does it automatically.
  • The title promises value (price + features + verdict), which earns the click.

Facts from the official source, always

  • Auto details (price, specs, features) come from the manufacturer's official website.
  • After ChatGPT writes, she matches every detail against the official source.
  • One wrong price or feature kills trust and hurts the site in Discover.

Why Auto Content Works So Well

  • New bike/car launches are constantly trending — a never-ending supply of topics.
  • Auto buyers actively search for price, features, and verdicts — real demand.
  • Official websites give verifiable facts, making articles safe to publish fast.
  • Auto articles appear in her Discover feeds again and again, which she turns into fresh content.
  • It's a category that scales — 90% of articles on Taza Time flow through her content system.

The Auto Article Checklist

  • Spot the trending model on multiple Discover feeds.
  • Open the manufacturer's official website for specs.
  • Draft the article with the facts you verified.
  • Use ChatGPT for the title — add price and features to make it clickable.
  • Verify every number against the official source before publishing.
  • Publish fast, while the trend is live.
