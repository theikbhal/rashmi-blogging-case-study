Blog Post · Volume · Production System

25–30 Posts Per Day

On her own blog alone, she publishes 25–30 articles every single day.

That's not a typo. Rashmi's blog gets 25–30 content pieces per day — and that's just her own blog, before the 13 websites she manages. Volume like this doesn't come from writing harder. It comes from a repeatable research → draft → AI → verify workflow and a team paid per article.

What "25–30 Per Day" Really Means

  • Her own blog publishes 25–30 articles daily, every day.
  • She manages 13 websites in total — this content system runs across all of them.
  • Writers who need more income produce 60–70 articles a day, working morning to night.
  • It's a system, not talent — a standard process repeated by trained writers.

The Production System

Step 1 — Find the trend (minutes)

  • Watch Google Discover on 3–4 phones at once.
  • When the same topic (e.g., a new bike) appears repeatedly across devices, that's the signal.
  • Pick the trending topic — it's already proven to get traffic.

Step 2 — Research the facts (fast)

  • Pull details from official websites (bike/car manufacturers, government sources).
  • No guessing — facts must be verifiable.
  • Speed comes from knowing exactly where to look.

Step 3 — Draft and polish with AI

  • Draft in Google Docs.
  • Polish with ChatGPT — titles, structure, readability.
  • Give ChatGPT the topic name and ask for attractive, clickable titles.
  • AI works — her whole site runs on it (90% of Taza Time articles go through her system).

Step 4 — Verify before publishing

  • Always match AI output against official sources.
  • Check price, features, dates — one wrong fact kills trust.
  • Then publish quickly while the topic is still trending.

How to Reach High Volume

  • Start with 1–3/day alone; the goal is a process, not a number.
  • Standardize the article format so anyone can follow it.
  • Add writers paid per article once income supports it — this is what turns 5/day into 25–30/day.
  • Reuse proven topics and drop weak ones; double down on winners.
  • Use AI for speed but verify every fact against an official source.
  • Volume only works if quality and accuracy hold — Discover punishes sloppy sites.
