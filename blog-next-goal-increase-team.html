Next Goal: Increase Team Size

The team doubled her income — so the next goal was more team.

Rashmi didn't stop at a few writers. When the team doubled her income, her next goal became clear: increase the team size. She reached out to friends' friends, kept training, and grew toward the 15-person team that now runs her content operation.

What Actually Happened

  • After the first team doubled income, she said: "the team is still small — I need more."
  • She didn't post ads. She contacted her team members' friends: "Brother, come join."
  • Recruitment spread through the network she already had.
  • She kept training every new person step by step.
  • The team grew gradually to 15 people, with ~₹1.5 lakh/month in writer payments.

Why Growing the Team Was the Right Goal

Proven model, more of it

  • The first hires had already doubled income — scaling the same model was low risk.
  • More writers → more articles → more websites managed → more income.
  • She could manage 13 websites because she had a team producing for all of them.

Hire through your network, not ads

  • Friends' friends came already trusted and motivated.
  • She knew the culture, and the team taught new people faster than she could alone.
  • Some of her best writers were people with real financial need — hiring them was responsibility and loyalty in one.

Each new person raised the ceiling

  • A bigger team meant she could move from writer to manager.
  • She separated management income from content income.
  • Growth goal led to an office, equipment, and a registered news operation.

How to Set This Goal Yourself

  • Set the next goal only after the current model is proven (income doubled once).
  • Define the target: "increase team size" — with a number and a timeline.
  • Recruit through people you trust; their friends come pre-vetted.
  • Scale training: build a playbook so anyone can train the next person.
  • Reinvest team profits into the team — equipment, laptops, office.
  • Every bigger team should mean more output and more income, not just more headcount.
