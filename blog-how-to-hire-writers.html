How to Hire Your First Writers

From roommates and friends to a 15-person team earning ₹1.5 lakh/month in salaries.

Rashmi didn't start with a hiring process. She started with the people around her — friends and roommates who watched her work. When profit appeared, she invited them in, trained them one by one, and paid per article. Here's the exact way she hired and scaled her team.

Her Hiring Timeline

  • While working alone, friends and roommates watched her build the blog.
  • When profit started, she said: "I need a team. Come join — I'll teach you everything step by step."
  • They agreed to work part-time, because everyone was also studying.
  • Once the team existed, income doubled.
  • She needed more people, so she contacted their friends.
  • She grew the team gradually to 15 people — paying ~₹1.5 lakh/month in total.
  • About 5–6 writers had real financial need — she even talked to their family members, took responsibility, and trained them properly. Today those writers earn ₹20,000–30,000/month each.

The Hiring Principles That Worked

Hire people you know, then trust

  • Friends and roommates already saw her consistency and understood the work.
  • No interviews, no resumes — just people nearby who were motivated.
  • Start with part-time; convert to more hours as output proves itself.

Train them step by step

  • She taught everything one by one — research, drafting, publishing.
  • Training created a repeatable standard instead of random quality.
  • Today writers follow her process: topic → official source → draft → AI polish → verify.

Pay per article, not salary

  • Writers are paid per article based on how many they write.
  • High-need, high-output writers do 60–70 articles a day, working morning to night.
  • Pay-for-output rewards productivity and scales with income — no fixed cost when output drops.

Motivation beats everything

  • She recruited people who needed and wanted the work.
  • For 5–6 writers in financial need, she took responsibility — and they became her best, most loyal writers.
  • Writer income (₹20k–30k/month) is proof the model works for the team too.

How to Hire Your First Writers

  • Don't hire strangers first — start with one person you trust who's seen you work.
  • Only hire when income/profit is already coming in; the team should pay for itself.
  • Start part-time and pay per article — low risk, high alignment.
  • Write a simple playbook: topic → source → draft → AI → verify → publish.
  • Train in person or on calls; show one full article end-to-end first.
  • Set a daily output target (e.g., 25–30 articles) that the team must hit.
  • Track writer earnings — happy, well-paid writers are your growth engine.
