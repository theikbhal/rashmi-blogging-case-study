Home Office Setup — A 2-BHK Flat as a Workspace

One room to live in, one room as the office where 7 people work.

Rashmi runs a ₹3 lakh/month content business from a rented 2-BHK flat. One bedroom is where people live; the other bedroom is the office where 7 team members work. No fancy coworking space, no big rent — a home office setup that kept costs low and output high.

What She Actually Did

  • She has a 2-BHK rented flat in Gorakhpur.
  • One room is where people live; the other room is the office.
  • 7 people work from that office room every day.
  • She bought laptops for her writers and a mic for herself.
  • Work runs from afternoon into late night — writers sometimes work until 2 AM.

Why This Setup Worked

It kept fixed costs near zero

  • No office rent, no commute, no infrastructure bills.
  • Living and working in one place meant expenses were one flat's rent.
  • Every rupee saved went back into the business and the team.

It built a team culture

  • Writers working side by side trained each other naturally.
  • Someone could ask a question without messaging or scheduling a call.
  • Urgent topics got handled instantly — the whole team is one room away.

It scaled the workflow

  • 7 people in one room, each paid per article, produced the volume behind 25–30 posts/day.
  • Laptops for writers removed the biggest equipment barrier.
  • Long hours (until 2 AM) were possible because the "office" never closed.

How to Set Up Your Own Home Office

  • Start with the space you already have — a room, a corner, a table.
  • Buy the minimum equipment that unlocks output (a laptop per writer).
  • Put the team physically together if you can — it accelerates training and speed.
  • Keep rent and fixed costs low so profits stay in the business.
  • Upgrade to a dedicated office only when revenue clearly pays for it.
