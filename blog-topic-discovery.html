Topic Discovery — Finding What to Write Every Day

"Topic research is easy. It depends on how you learn it."

Writing 25–30 posts a day only works if you never run out of ideas. Rashmi's topic discovery system solves that: watch Google Discover on multiple phones, spot the topic that keeps repeating, verify it on official sites, and write it fast. Here's the full framework, step by step.

Her Full Topic Discovery System

  • Keep 3–4 phones and check Google Discover on each.
  • Look for a topic that appears repeatedly across all devices — that's a confirmed trend.
  • Example: a new Yamaha bike keeps surfacing on every feed → she picks that topic.
  • Collect the details from official websites (manufacturer, government, source sites).
  • Draft, polish with AI, verify facts, and publish quickly.
  • Repeat daily — the trend feeds the machine.

Why This System Works

Google already did the research

  • Discover surfaces articles that are already getting clicks and attention.
  • A topic repeating on multiple devices means it's trending — not a fluke.
  • She lets the algorithm pick topics instead of guessing.

Old topics resurface — and that's gold

  • Some articles in Discover are 2 years old, suddenly appearing again.
  • If an old article is trending, a fresh one on the same topic can ride the same wave.
  • Spotting these resurgences is a hidden advantage of daily feed watching.

Facts from official sources keep quality high

  • Trending topics attract competition — accuracy decides who keeps traffic.
  • Official websites give verifiable details she can trust and cite.
  • Verified + fast = the combination Discover rewards.

Topic Discovery Checklist

  • Open Discover on 3–4 devices at the same time.
  • Note any topic that appears on all of them.
  • Check for resurfacing old articles on the same topic.
  • Collect facts from the official website only.
  • Draft in Google Docs, polish with AI, verify, publish fast.
  • Log what trended so you can repeat the winners.
