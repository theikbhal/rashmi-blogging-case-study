Deendayal Upadhyay Gorakhpur University

NAAC A++ · established 1957 · the university where Rashmi studies

Deen Dayal Upadhyay Gorakhpur University — informally known as Gorakhpur University — is the state university where Rashmi Gupta is pursuing her B.A. in Psychology (final year) while running her news blogging and content-writing business. It's the academic hub that brought her from Gopalganj, Bihar to Gorakhpur.

About the University

Identity & motto

  • Officially Deen Dayal Upadhyay Gorakhpur University, commonly called Gorakhpur University.
  • Motto: "Let noble thoughts come to us from all directions."
  • A teaching, residential-cum-affiliating state university in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh.
  • Among the top five state universities in the country after earning NAAC Grade A++ — the first in the state to score 3.78.
  • Located ~2 km east of downtown, almost walking distance from the railway station.

History

  • The idea of a residential university at Gorakhpur began with C. J. Rao, principal of St. Andrews College (then under Agra University), who started PG and UG science teaching.
  • Shaped by S. N. M. Tripathi and approved in principle by Uttar Pradesh's first Chief Minister, Govind Ballabh Pant.
  • Established by an act of the U.P. Legislature in 1956; began functioning on 1 September 1957.
  • Faculties of Arts, Commerce, Law and Education started first; Science followed in 1958; Engineering, Medicine and Agriculture came later.

Research & Recognition

Strong departments

  • Physics: research in spectroscopy, semiconductor devices, thin-film solar cells, and particle physics — including collaborative work with CERN.
  • Chemistry: famous for solid-state processes, thermodynamics, and non-linear dynamics under Prof. R. P. Rastogi.
  • Mathematics & Statistics: relativity, differential geometry, fluid dynamics, number theory, and Bayesian inference.

Notable alumni

  • Rajnath Singh — Defence Minister of India.
  • Sanjaya Rajaram — World Food Prize winner (2014).
  • Meenakshi Narain — physicist at Brown University; contributed to top quark and Higgs Boson discoveries.
  • Vir Bahadur Singh, Mata Prasad Pandey, Ram Govind Chaudhary, and many other leaders and scientists.

The Rashmi Gupta Connection

Why this university matters to her story

  • Rashmi moved from Gopalganj, Bihar to Gorakhpur to study here.
  • She is in her final year of B.A. Psychology and plans to shift to Mass Communication/Journalism next.
  • Her college day runs ~6–7 hours; from 4 PM to 3–4 AM she works on her blogging business.
  • The university's student community is exactly where she found her roommates, friends, and first 15-person team.
  • Gorakhpur's role as an education hub (affordable hostels, libraries, part-time work) made this balance possible.

Study + business balance

  • She treats college as the morning block and blogging as the evening/night block.
  • Her team works from a 2-BHK flat office while she manages it between classes.
  • She proves you don't choose between education and building — you schedule both.
